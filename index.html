Search Funnels (Google + AI)

Build authority around the service or product you most want to be found for.

We research the searches, questions, comparisons, and AI prompts around that subject, then build a connected group of pages that support one main Pillar Page. Those supporting pages help people find you in more ways while strengthening the depth, relevance, and credibility of the page you most want Google and AI to rank and recommend.

Here’s how we do it

Five steps, from research to a full year of growth, all built around the one page you most want Google and AI to recommend.

01

Plan your Search Funnel

We learn your business, customers, and competitors, then research how people look for your service or product across Google and AI.

From that, we choose the first service or product to build around based on business value, search demand, competition, and opportunity.

02

Build your Business Fact Page

Before we write anything, we create one approved source of truth for your business.

It captures everything that proves who you are: your legal and public names, when you were founded and how long you’ve been established, your locations and service areas, your services and products, and the credentials behind them: licenses, certifications, insurance and bonding, contract vehicles and set-aside statuses, professional memberships and affiliations. It also records your awards, your longest-standing clients, notable projects and results you’re cleared to name, and the way you want each of these stated.

We use it as the reference for everything we create so your published content stays accurate and consistent, and so Google and AI see the same facts about you everywhere.

03

Build your Pillar Page

We choose one core service or product page and build it around the most important searches, questions, comparisons, and AI prompts related to that subject.

That page becomes the center of the funnel.

04

Add five supporting pages

We build five supporting pages around the Pillar Page, each focused on an important question, comparison, problem, or search.

They may be service pages, resource pages, blog articles, cost guides, comparison pages, or FAQs. They do not all need to appear in your main menu.

Each one links back to the Pillar Page, giving people more ways to find you while also strengthening the subject around the page you most want to rank.

05

Grow and optimize the funnel throughout the year

Over the following year, we add about 10 more supporting pages based on new searches, AI prompts, competitor gaps, and customer questions.

Some pages may support the Pillar Page directly. Others may support one of the original five pages and flow back through it.
